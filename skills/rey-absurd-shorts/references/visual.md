# Original blocky 3D visual direction

Use chunky toy-like proportions, simple materials and expressive staging that evoke a playful block-building game world without reproducing proprietary Roblox avatars, maps, UI, logos, animations or sounds.

## Character system

- Use a compact reusable rig: head, torso, upper/lower arms, hands, upper/lower legs and simple face planes.
- Separate characters through silhouette first, then color, hair/hat shape and one prop. Avoid detailed textures that turn noisy on mobile.
- Create at least five useful expressions: neutral, determined, worried, shocked and exhausted. Add blink and mouth states.
- Acting requires pose changes: anticipation, action and settle. Mouth flaps alone do not count.
- Keep feet grounded and give walk cycles weight. Add small torso lean, arm counter-swing and head focus.

## Scene variety

For 90 seconds, aim for 10–14 materially different setups. Rotate among:

- town-wide establishing shot;
- medium character dialogue/action;
- over-the-shoulder decision shot;
- environmental insert or warning detail;
- overhead map or crowd pattern;
- monster point of view;
- silhouette reveal;
- scale-comparison wide shot;
- chase side view;
- close reaction and final punchline tableau.

Do not leave one character in one location while backgrounds, captions or light colors change. Every setup should reveal new geography, action, information or emotion.

## Camera and lighting

- Use readable 28–50 mm-equivalent compositions for most shots; reserve wide distortion for deliberate comedy or threat.
- Stage foreground, subject and background in clear layers. Maintain headroom for captions around 70% frame height.
- Use simple key/fill/rim separation. Change lighting as a story event only when the world state changes.
- Introduce the monster as sound/shadow/environmental response before a clean silhouette, then a controlled full reveal.

## Mobile QA

Review stills at phone size. Faces, hands, props and the monster silhouette must remain readable. Keep captions away from faces and platform UI. Remove tiny set dressing, unexplained markers and decorative effects that do not advance the story.
