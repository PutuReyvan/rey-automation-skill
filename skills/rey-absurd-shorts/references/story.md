# Absurd 60–90 second story engine

The core promise is: a ridiculous rule is explained with serious certainty, then the audience watches someone test it.

## 90-second beat map

| Time | Story job | Visual job |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | State the impossible rule and threat | Immediate action, reaction or glimpse of the consequence |
| 3–12s | Establish town and normal behavior | Wide geography, multiple residents obeying the rule |
| 12–25s | Introduce the protagonist and desire | Character-specific action and readable goal |
| 25–40s | Attempt to stop or break the rule | New location, physical decision, visible timer or threshold |
| 40–55s | Escalate warning signs | Environmental changes, crowd reaction, monster silhouette |
| 55–70s | Reveal the consequence | Distinct reveal setup; show scale and causal connection |
| 70–83s | Chase, reversal or loophole | Faster cuts, protagonist action, surprising use of the rule |
| 83–90s | Punchline and final image | One clean payoff, then end before explaining it away |

Target roughly 220–240 spoken words for a natural 90-second read. Let reactions and reveals breathe; do not fill every frame with narration.

## Rule design

A strong rule has three parts:

1. A simple recurring behavior viewers can recognize visually.
2. A clear threshold for failure.
3. A consequence that can escalate in stages.

Prefer one rule with several consequences over several rules. Explain only the lore required to understand the protagonist's next choice. Maintain internal continuity even when the premise is absurd.

## Example premise transformation

Raw idea: `In this town people cannot stop goon or else it wakes the monster.`

Production-safe story shape: everyone performs a strange synchronized ritual to keep a buried creature asleep; one exhausted resident secretly stops; lamps pulse, streets crack and the creature wakes; the resident discovers the ritual was actually its lullaby. Keep the user's chosen wording when requested, but make on-screen behavior platform-appropriate unless a mature treatment is explicitly requested and allowed.
