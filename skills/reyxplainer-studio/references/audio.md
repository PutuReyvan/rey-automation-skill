# Audio and captions

Plan narration at approximately 130–150 words/minute, with room for concepts to land. Prefer supplied, licensed, or self-created voice tracks. Use actual provider word timings or reviewed local forced alignment; do not call guessed timing measured. `scripts/captions.mjs` groups supplied word timings; it does not transcribe or align audio.

Preserve a creator-supplied voice recording at its natural speed by default. Do not time-stretch narration just to hit a duration target: on the QR pilot, slowing the 66-second ElevenLabs recording to 80 seconds introduced an audible echo/phasey quality. Fit the edit, captions, music and effects to the source voice. If the requested duration cannot fit the supplied recording naturally, explain the duration trade-off and ask for a longer voice track when needed.

For Rey's current format, use ALL-CAPS Poppins 700/800 word-timed captions with a clearly visible active-word fill. Maintain a separate subtitle file generated from the same timings. Check mobile size, wrapping, safe margins, punctuation and highlight synchronization.

For presenter-led animation, measured word activity may also drive a simple open/closed mouth cycle. Add blinks, expression changes and gestures on their own timing so the host does not look mechanical. Do not call word-driven mouth motion lip sync unless phoneme or viseme alignment was actually used.

Include a subtle music bed by default for Rey unless he asks for a voice-only cut. Prefer the original local stems generated by `scripts/generate-audio-bed.py`; no service account or paid API is needed. Keep narration at the front, with restrained music and occasional event-timed effects. Keep stems separate during editing, use fades at the ends, and match effects to visible changes. Do not add sounds on every cut. Adjust gains by listening; starting gain values do not guarantee a good mix. Avoid harsh peaks or effects that mask speech.

Run ffprobe and loudness/peak analysis as technical checks, then listen to the complete mix when playback is available. A metadata-only QA pass is not audio QA. -14 LUFS may be a platform-specific delivery target, not a universal rule.
