# Changelog

## v0.2.1 — semantic visual and presenter patch

- Turned the recurring host into an active presenter with scene-specific gestures, expressions, body motion and honest word-timed mouth animation.
- Added a presenter-led invariant: keep the host in every scene unless the creator explicitly approves a clean cutaway.
- Required status cards, decoded values, labels, magnified views and exploded views to identify and connect to their source.
- Required highlights to match actual mechanism geometry; QR modules and finder patterns must be shown at their true positions and proportions.
- Promoted diagram counts, colors and legends to correctness checks and added targeted still-review gates for the opening, failure/recovery states and data comparisons.
- Made the bundled core test resolve both the packaged template and an initialized project's `src/episode.json`, so the skill can be tested before and after initialization.

## v0.2 — visual direction update after QR pilot feedback

- Recorded Rey's review that the QR pilot felt static because it stayed on one centered 3D board and relied on changing highlights.
- Made scene variety explicit: plan distinct shot setups for each major beat, including close-ups, breakdowns, context, diagrams, and consequences. Color-only changes do not qualify as new scenes.
- Added a storyboard/contact-sheet review gate and the new Instagram scene-variety reference supplied by Rey.
- Recorded Rey's request for a toon 3D person/host in the QR regeneration, with expressive reactions and clean diagram-only beats where a character would distract.
- Recorded the QR pilot audio failure: stretching the supplied 66-second VO to 80 seconds caused audible echo/phasey artifacts. Future cuts should preserve supplied narration speed and fit edit/audio stems to it.
